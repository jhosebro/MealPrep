# MealPrep — Product Overview

MealPrep is a personal pantry and meal planning mobile app for iOS and Android (with web support). It helps users track what's in their fridge, manage grocery budgets, generate AI-powered recipe suggestions based on available ingredients, and maintain shopping lists.

## Core Features

- **Fridge/Pantry tracking** — add, edit, and monitor ingredient stock with quantity, unit, category, status (available / low / empty), expiry dates, and estimated days remaining
- **Recipe generation** — suggests recipes (desayuno, almuerzo, cena) based on current fridge contents, highlights missing ingredients
- **Saved recipes** — users can bookmark generated recipes for later
- **Shopping list** — tracks items to buy with budget awareness
- **Budget tracking** — records grocery spending per store visit
- **Authentication** — email/password sign-up and sign-in with biometric unlock support

## Target Users

Individual users managing their own household groceries. The app UI is in Spanish (Colombian locale — store names, categories, and units reflect local context).

## Key Domain Constants

- **Categories**: frutas, verduras, carnes, pescados, lacteos, granos, condimentos, cereales, ocasionales, otros
- **Units**: und, paq, kg, g, lb, oz, L, ml, cup, tbsp, tsp
- **Stores**: Exito, D1, Ara, Surtifamiliar, El hato, Otro
- **Meal types**: desayuno, almuerzo, cena
- **Item statuses**: available, low, empty
